# Custocare — One Pager

**Continuous Care. Clinical Excellence.**  
Custocare is a facility-wide platform (Web & desktop clients + secure backend) designed so **care stays coherent across every touchpoint**—from registration through clinical decision-making, pharmacy, lab, nursing, and revenue—with **consistent standards** teams can rely on.

---

## Who we serve

Hospitals, clinics, and medical centers that want **continuous care without operational seams**: same patient story across visits and departments, **clinical excellence** supported by structured workflows—not fragmented tools for registration, wards, prescribing, labs, and billing.

## What Custocare delivers

| Pillar | In one line |
|--------|-------------|
| **Patient & visit** | A connected journey—queues, encounters, and context that carries forward visit to visit. |
| **Clinical** | Documentation and decisions aligned to **clinical excellence**: consultations, vitals, notes, diagnosis—built for quality and clarity. |
| **Pharmacy & lab** | Safe, traceable prescribing, dispensing, and lab flows tied to the **same clinical thread**. |
| **Nursing & wards** | **Continuous care** at the bedside—beds, meds, tasks, and handovers that preserve accountability. |
| **Revenue** | Service catalog, billing cycles, invoices, refunds—financial clarity grounded in real clinical activity. |
| **Administration** | Facilities, departments, staff, roles—so permissions and governance match how **excellent care** is delivered. |

## Why it matters

- **Continuous Care** — fewer gaps between encounters, departments, and shifts; context follows the patient.  
- **Clinical Excellence** — workflows that support consistent, auditable, team-based practice—not heroic workarounds.  
- **Clearer cash flow** through billing tied to real clinical and service activity.  
- **Built to scale** from a single site to structured multi-facility operation—same standards, broader footprint.

## Business model (summary)

**Revenue:** facility subscriptions (module and scale tiers), implementation and onboarding, training and premium support; payment-related flows where applicable.  
**Costs:** product/engineering, cloud and reliability, compliance and security, customer success, sales and partnerships.
