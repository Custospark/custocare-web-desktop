## Slide 2 — Problem: Facilities run on fragments

- Siloed tools for registration, charts, dispensing, labs, and billing slow everyone down—and **break continuity of care**.
- Cash leakage when services aren’t captured, billed, or reconciled consistently.
- Weak handoffs between clinical, pharmacy, nursing, and admin teams—**context drops between shifts** and departments.
- Operational drag from duplicate entry, queues, and limited visibility—harder to sustain **clinical excellence** at scale.

*Bridge:* Custocare replaces patchwork with **one operational layer for Continuous Care**—without sacrificing **Clinical Excellence**.

---

## Slide 3 — Solution: One platform for continuous care and outcomes

- Single source of truth so the **patient story carries forward** across visits, roles, and departments.
- Role-based workspaces that align physicians, nurses, pharmacy, lab, and operations around **shared clinical standards**.
- Facility-ready deployment: desktop client plus secure backend APIs—built for **daily excellence**, not shelfware.

## Slide 5 — Capability map: End-to-end modules

- **Patient & visits** — Registration, lifecycle, queues, summaries—**continuity from door to discharge**  
- **Clinical** — Consultations, vitals, notes, diagnoses, documents—**excellence by design**  
- **Pharmacy & inventory** — Prescriptions, dispensing, encounter-linked meds—**safe, traceable therapy**  
- **Lab** — Requests and dashboards aligned to visits and billing—**results in the clinical thread**  
- **Nursing & wards** — Beds, tasks, medications, dosing, shift handovers—**continuous care at the bedside**  
- **Admin & platform** — Facilities, wards, departments, staff, invitations—**who can do what, and why**
